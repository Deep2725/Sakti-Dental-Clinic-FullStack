Our Founder

Dr. Anupriya

Dr. Anupriya brings over 20 years of dental experience. She graduated in 2000, worked for six years at Mathura Clinic, and established Sakthi Dental Clinic in Hosur in 2004.


She has also served as a dental consultant with the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital for over a decade. The clinic combines modern dental technology with a warm, patient-friendly environment.
