FAQs

Common dental questions

Can medication completely relieve tooth pain?

Medication may provide short-term relief, but lasting relief requires identifying and treating the underlying cause.

Does teeth scaling damage enamel or create gaps?

No. Scaling removes plaque and tartar. Temporary sensitivity can occur while gums recover.

Can fluorosis stains be removed through scaling?

Scaling can remove surface stains, but fluorosis is an internal stain and may require cosmetic treatment.

Can painful teeth be treated with fillings?

Not always. If decay reaches the pulp, root canal treatment or extraction may be needed.

Is a crown necessary after root canal treatment?

A crown is commonly recommended to protect a treated tooth and improve durability.

Can milk teeth need root canal treatment?

Yes, when a deep cavity affects a milk tooth that needs to remain functional for some time.

How can I maintain good oral hygiene?

Brush twice daily, rinse after meals, floss regularly and attend routine dental check-ups.

What is the best age to get braces?

Braces are often effective around ages 12–14, though timing depends on the individual case.

Do all wisdom teeth need to be removed?

No. Removal depends on problems such as impaction, infection, pain or damage to nearby teeth.

Is it important to replace missing teeth after extraction?

Replacement can help maintain bite alignment, chewing function and tooth position.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Implants can restore the appearance and function of missing teeth for suitable patients.

Which toothbrush and toothpaste should I use?

A soft or medium-bristled small-head toothbrush and non-abrasive fluoride toothpaste are recommended.

What causes tooth sensitivity?

Possible causes include enamel wear, decay, exposed roots and small cracks in teeth.

How often should I see a dentist?

Routine dental visits every six months are recommended for preventive care.
